    Forecast

    Shaded band is the 95% interval. The dotted line shows how the model would have done over the last 12 months. Use the scenario slider to test a better or worse outlook.

    Model: additive damped trend with 12-month seasonality (Holt-Winters), fitted on 2012 onward and precomputed in Python. Intervals widen with horizon and are indicative. The scenario slider simply scales the forecast up or down; it is not a second model. The forecast reflects review activity, not true sales.
